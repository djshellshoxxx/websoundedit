LOCAL AUDIO RESEARCH

Neural stem isolation · prototype

Separate a short music clip into vocals, drums, bass and other instruments with a neural model. Audio is processed in this tab on your device.

This experimental workflow is separate from the editor’s stereo center/side presets. Model estimates can contain bleed and artifacts. Use a capable desktop; model inference may need more than 1 GB of memory.

01 / MODEL

Load the research model

Select the supported htdemucs_fp16weights.onnx file from your device. The model’s size and SHA-256 are checked before it runs.

The model is not bundled or downloaded automatically. Read the model card and prototype notes for the tested file and weight provenance.

No model selected.

02 / SOURCE

Choose a short WAV

Use a mono or stereo PCM/float WAV up to 30 seconds and 24 MiB. Export a region from WebSoundEdit if your track is longer.

Input is resampled to 44.1 kHz. Mono is duplicated for the model. Surround files are rejected. Normalize below full scale in the editor if the source peaks above 0 dBFS.

03 / SEPARATE

Create the stems

Inference runs in a dedicated worker. Cancel stops that worker and releases the model; load it again to retry.

Choose a model and a source WAV to begin.

04 / LISTEN & EXPORT

Your separated stems

Downloads preserve the model’s output as stereo, 44.1 kHz, 32-bit float WAV. Listening previews use 16-bit PCM and clamp peaks above full scale. Import the downloads into WebSoundEdit to edit or mix them.

Instrumental is the sum of the drums, bass and other predictions. Stem sums may differ from the original recording.
